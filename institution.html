The institution

Human well-being, approached as a science and a system.

Built in India, architected for global relevance.

Identity

Body & Science is being established as an integrated human science institution. Its purpose is to understand the human body, improve health and develop human capabilities through scientific knowledge and practical systems.

Vision

A world in which every individual has access to the scientific knowledge, resources and capabilities required to maintain and improve health.

Mission

To convert human science into practical, affordable and scalable systems that improve human well-being.

Philosophy

The human body is a biological system. Its health must be understood scientifically, developed systematically and sustained economically.

Motto

Understand. Develop. Sustain.

Institutional objective

Our objective is not to build a single successful business. It is to establish an enduring institution capable of creating, improving and sustaining multiple solutions for human well-being.

How the institution creates value

  1. Understand: generate, evaluate and organise reliable knowledge.
  2. Develop: translate knowledge into education, skills, services and useful products.
  3. Sustain: build responsible systems and economic activities that support long-term access and institutional continuity.
The institutional identity and frameworks on this page express the founding direction. Formal legal objects and governing documents will be finalised through the appropriate Indian legal and regulatory process.
