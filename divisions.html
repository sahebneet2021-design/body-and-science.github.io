Institutional architecture

Seven divisions. Shared standards. One mission.

The proposed divisions connect discovery, learning, human development and delivery.

01

Science & Research

Human physiology, nutrition, food science, exercise science, behavioural science and evidence evaluation.

02

Knowledge & Education

Public education, learning resources, curricula, courses and educator development.

03

Human Development

Develop capabilities in learners, educators, mentors, operators and institutional leaders.

04

Wellness & Health Services

Responsible nutrition education, fitness, recovery and wellness programmes, with regulated clinical work delivered by qualified professionals.

05

Nutrition Products & Manufacturing

Evidence-informed everyday nutrition products, formulation, quality systems and responsible manufacturing.

06

Agriculture & Supply Infrastructure

Responsible sourcing, cultivation, processing, storage, traceability and circular resource systems.

07

Technology & Infrastructure

Digital systems for knowledge, research, learning, wellness, products, supply, finance and institutional operations.

Shared institutional services

Finance, legal and compliance, people operations, quality assurance, procurement, communications, data governance and institutional administration support all divisions.

How the divisions connect

Research informs knowledge. Knowledge develops people. People improve services and products. Responsible delivery produces learning and resources that support further research and public benefit.

These are proposed organisational divisions, not a statement that all departments are currently staffed or operational.
