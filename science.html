Science & research

Evidence is the foundation. Translation is the work.

We aim to connect reliable human science with decisions, education, products and programmes that can be tested in the real world.

Evidence before claims

Differentiate established findings, emerging evidence, plausible mechanisms and untested hypotheses.

Safety before scale

Quality, safety and regulatory compliance are conditions for responsible delivery, not optional stages.

Transparency about limits

Communicate uncertainty, limitations, methods and relevant conflicts of interest.

Measure real outcomes

Evaluate whether knowledge and interventions improve understanding, behaviour, access or relevant health outcomes.

Research domains

  • Human physiology and metabolic health
  • Nutrition science and dietary patterns
  • Food science, formulation and product quality
  • Exercise science, physical capability and recovery
  • Behavioural science, learning and adherence
  • Public health, affordability and access

Research integrity

Research involving people, identifiable personal data, biological samples or clinical interventions must receive the required ethical review, consent, privacy protections and regulatory approvals before it begins. This website does not announce any ongoing approved human-subject research.

Current stage: The research agenda and evidence standards are being developed. No completed research findings or formal institutional research approvals are being claimed on this page.
