Governance & transparency

Stewardship must be stronger than individual interests.

The institution's credibility depends on clear authority, independent oversight, responsible finance and public accountability.

Proposed governance framework

Governing body

Protects the institution's purpose, approves strategy and budgets, appoints accountable leadership and oversees continuity.

Scientific and ethics oversight

Reviews evidence standards, research integrity, safety, ethics, conflicts of interest and scientific claims. Scientific safety decisions should not be overridden by commercial targets.

Executive management

Translates approved strategy into annual plans, budgets, operations, people development and measurable outcomes.

Non-profit economic model

Body & Science intends to fund its mission through a combination of grants, donations, partnerships and mission-aligned economic activities such as products, education and services. Surplus is intended to be reinvested in the institution's objects, not distributed as private profit.

Commercial activity must be conducted through a legally appropriate structure, with separate and accurate accounting, required tax treatment, controls over conflicts of interest and compliance with all applicable laws.

Transparency commitments

  • Publish the institution's governing documents and material policies once formally adopted.
  • Publish annual activity and financial reports when available and approved.
  • Explain material corrections and document revisions.
  • Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and safeguards.
  • Protect personal, confidential and legally restricted information.

Public reporting status

No audited financial statements, registration certificates, tax exemption approvals or annual institutional reports are published here at this founding stage. They will be added only when they exist and are authorised for public release.

Legal structure: The intended non-profit model requires specialist advice under Indian law. A Section 8 company may be one possible form, but suitability, objects, permitted economic activities, tax treatment and compliance must be assessed by qualified professionals before registration. This site does not represent that registration has occurred.
