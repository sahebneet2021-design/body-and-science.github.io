Understand. Develop. Sustain.

Our reason for existing

Science should improve everyday human life.

Scientific knowledge should not remain confined to laboratories, academic institutions or privileged populations. It must be translated into education, nutrition, responsible services and systems that people can use.

Institutional philosophy

The human body is a biological system.

Its health must be understood scientifically, developed systematically and sustained economically. We connect knowledge, people and responsible economic activity in service of human well-being.

Three central missions

One integrated purpose.

01

Understand the human body

Build and communicate reliable knowledge across physiology, nutrition, movement, behaviour and the factors that shape health.

02

Improve human health

Translate evidence into accessible nutrition, education, products and responsible programmes that support better everyday decisions.

03

Develop human capabilities

Equip individuals and professionals with the knowledge, skills and systems needed to sustain meaningful improvements.

Institutional foundations

Five foundations. One coherent institution.

Each foundation reinforces the others. Together they make the institution capable of learning, acting and enduring.

Our approach

Build an institution, not a collection of products.

Research informs education. Education develops people. People build better services and products. Responsible economic activity helps fund further research, access and institutional continuity.

Our commitment

Evidence before claims.
