# Body & Science — Constitutional Charter

> **Legal notice:** This is a public founding charter, not a Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association, trust deed or other legally operative registration document. It does not establish legal personality, tax exemption, statutory recognition or regulatory approval. The final legal constitution must be prepared and adopted with qualified Indian legal, tax and compliance advisers.

## Preamble

Body & Science is founded on the belief that scientific understanding of the human body should lead to practical improvements in human life. Knowledge must be made understandable, capability must be developed, and solutions must be made accessible and sustainable.

The institution seeks to connect research, education, human development and responsible economic activity in a continuing cycle of learning and service.

## Article I — Name and character

The institution shall be known as **Body & Science**. It is intended to operate as a non-profit institution dedicated to human science, health, education and capability development. Its legal form and registered name shall be determined through applicable law and formal registration.

## Article II — Purpose

The institution exists to understand the human body, improve human health and develop human capabilities through science-based knowledge, education, products, services and systems.

## Article III — Core objects

1. Advance and communicate knowledge relating to human physiology, nutrition, food, movement, behaviour and well-being.
2. Support scientific research, evidence evaluation and responsible translation of findings into practice.
3. Provide accessible education and develop the capabilities of learners, educators and professionals.
4. Develop, evaluate and make available safe, useful and affordable nutrition-related products and services, subject to applicable standards and regulation.
5. Build partnerships and infrastructure that strengthen responsible sourcing, production, distribution and access.
6. Promote public understanding of health while respecting evidence, dignity, privacy and applicable law.

## Article IV — Foundational principles

### Scientific integrity
Claims must be proportionate to evidence. Methods, limitations, uncertainty and conflicts of interest should be disclosed where relevant. Evidence must not be distorted to serve commercial or reputational interests.

### Human dignity and access
People must be treated with respect. The institution should work to make useful knowledge and solutions more accessible, including to communities with limited resources.

### Safety and responsibility
Safety, quality, informed participation and compliance take precedence over speed or commercial advantage. Regulated clinical activities shall be conducted only by appropriately qualified persons and entities.

### Independence and accountability
Scientific evaluation, financial oversight and governance should have defined responsibilities. Decisions should be documented and conflicts of interest managed transparently.

### Continuous improvement
Policies and operating procedures should evolve through evidence, measured outcomes, review and documented learning. Changes affecting safety, quality or compliance require appropriate approval.

## Article V — Non-profit economic model

The institution intends to fund its mission through grants, donations, partnerships and income generated by mission-aligned products, services, education, research and other lawful economic activities.

All income and property shall be applied solely towards the institution's objects and lawful obligations. No part of the institution's surplus or property shall be distributed directly or indirectly to founders, members, directors, trustees or other private persons by way of profit, dividend or personal benefit, except for reasonable and properly authorised payments for genuine services or reimbursements permitted by law.

Any business or trading activity must be structured, accounted for and taxed in accordance with applicable law. The institution shall obtain professional advice on the legal conditions for carrying on economic activities and claiming any available tax treatment.

## Article VI — Knowledge and public benefit

The institution should make appropriate educational resources, public-interest research outputs, policies and institutional reports available to the public. Publication must respect privacy, intellectual property, research ethics, confidentiality and applicable law.

## Article VII — Governance framework

The proposed governance framework includes a governing board or equivalent lawful governing body, independent scientific and ethics oversight, executive management and documented financial and operational controls. The actual organs, powers, appointment procedures and fiduciary duties shall be defined in the legally adopted governing instruments.

## Article VIII — Stewardship of resources

Capital shall be allocated according to mission relevance, evidence, need, affordability, operational capability, risk and long-term sustainability. The institution should maintain adequate reserves, transparent accounts and appropriate internal controls.

## Article IX — Review and amendment

This charter may be revised through a documented process approved by the institution's authorised governing body once constituted. Amendments must preserve the non-profit purpose, scientific integrity and public-benefit orientation, and must not conflict with applicable law or the legally adopted governing instruments.

## Article X — Founding commitment

Body & Science commits to building an institution in which knowledge generates capability, capability generates responsible value, and value sustains the continuous development and application of knowledge for human well-being.
